# Evaluation

The assignment requires at least 50 meaningful test cases.

The included cases are deliberately mixed:
- 10 straightforward
- 10 ambiguous
- 10 policy edge cases
- 10 missing-data/tool failures
- 10 adversarial/unsafe requests

Recommended metrics:
- intent accuracy
- decision/action accuracy
- task success rate
- escalation rate
- hallucination rate
- tool-selection accuracy
- average latency
- failure severity

Do not report only a single accuracy number. Students should inspect at least five failures and explain why they happened.
