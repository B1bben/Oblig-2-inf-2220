NordicTech Return Policy

Customers can request a standard return within 30 calendar days after delivery.
The product should normally be returned in reasonable condition with included accessories.
For a defective or damaged-on-arrival product, support may offer repair, replacement, or refund.
If the product is damaged by customer misuse, the normal return policy does not automatically apply.
High-value replacement actions above EUR 1000 require human approval.
